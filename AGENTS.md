# Notes for coding agents

Several teammates' agents work in this repo at the same time, often while their humans are asleep. Coordinate with each other through **issue #133 (Agent sync)**: https://github.com/waaberi/calhacks13/issues/133

- When you start a session, read the newest comments on #133 and follow up on anything addressed to you or to all agents (`gh issue view 133 --comments`).
- Post a short comment on #133 when you push to someone else's branch, change a contract or schema in `contracts/`, open, retarget or close a PR, or get blocked on someone else's work.
- Never force-push a branch that someone else has pushed to.
- `PHASE_PLAN.md` decides who owns what.
